Account & Data Deletion — Amal Tracker (আমল ট্র্যাকার)

Effective date: 7 January 2026

App package: com.amaltracker.app

This page explains how you can request deletion of your Amal Tracker account and associated cloud data. For help, contact: effttech@gmail.com

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to effttech@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign in to the App.
  2. Use the subject: Delete my Amal Tracker account
  3. In the message, include: your account email and (optional) a screenshot of the Profile page showing you’re logged in.

What will be deleted

  • Cloud data linked to your account (Firebase/Firestore), such as prayer tracking, dhikr counts, daily amal checklist, reading logs, sin tracker data, and custom reminders.
  • Account authentication record (Firebase Authentication), if applicable/possible after verification.

What will NOT be deleted automatically

  • Local device data stored on your phone (offline database). To remove it, uninstall the app or clear app storage in Android settings.

Deletion timeline

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days after verifying the request. Some records may be retained if required by law or for security purposes.
